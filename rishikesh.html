Rishikesh - Yoga Capital Of The World

The Yoga Capital of the World

Rishikesh, located on the banks of the sacred Ganga River in Uttarakhand, is a serene town known for its spiritual significance, natural beauty, and adventure activities. It holds a revered place in Hindu mythology and has been a center for sages and saints since ancient times. The town gained global recognition when The Beatles visited the ashram of Maharishi Mahesh Yogi in 1968, sparking Western interest in yoga and meditation. Known as the “Gateway to the Garhwal Himalayas,” Rishikesh is dotted with temples, ashrams, and ghats, and is also a hub for river rafting, trekking, and camping. Its blend of spirituality and adventure draws both pilgrims and international travelers seeking peace and purpose. Apart from its spiritual charm, Rishikesh is also an adventure hub—famous for white-water rafting, bungee jumping, and trekking. Landmarks like Laxman Jhula, Ram Jhula, and Parmarth Niketan add to its cultural and religious significance. Alcohol and non-vegetarian food are banned in the town, reflecting its sacred status.

Fantastic Facts

Triveni Ghat is the largest and most sacred bathing ghat in Rishikesh, where three rivers—Ganga, Yamuna, and Saraswati—are believed to meet spiritually. It is renowned for the Ganga Aarti held every evening, a mesmerizing ritual with chanting, lamps, and devotional music that draws both devotees and tourists. Pilgrims take a holy dip here to cleanse their sins, and the ghat is also lined with temples, sadhus, and small shrines, making it a powerful spiritual hub in the city.

Laxman Jhula

Iconic iron suspension bridge believed to be where Lord Laxman crossed the Ganga
5 km from Rishikesh town center
October to March – pleasant weather for sightseeing and walking.

Trivani Ghat

Holiest bathing ghat where Ganga, Yamuna, and Saraswati are believed to meet spiritually. Famous for daily Ganga Aarti at sunset.
Main Rishikesh town, near Rishikesh Railway Station
October to March – attend the evening aarti for a spiritual experience.

Neer Garh Waterfall

A beautiful multi-level natural waterfall with crystal-clear water, perfect for short treks and nature photography.
Around 5 km from Laxman Jhula on the Badrinath Highway
September to February – post-monsoon the waterfall is at its best.

River Rafting

Rishikesh is one of India’s top white-water rafting destinations
Starts from Shivpuri, Marine Drive, or Brahmpuri and ends in Rishikesh
October to June(closed during peak monsoon months, July–August)

👜 Shopping: Shop for Banarasi silk sarees, wooden toys, perfumes (ittar), and brassware. Best places: Vishwanath Gali, Thatheri Bazaar.
🚕 Transport: Use auto-rickshaws or walk in the narrow lanes. Cycle rickshaws and boats are popular near the ghats. Avoid driving yourself.
⚠️ Safety: Keep belongings secure, beware of pickpockets in crowded areas, and politely decline persistent touts. Drink bottled water only.
📅 Best Time: November–February. Pleasant weather and major festivals like Dev Deepawali make it ideal for visits.
🎟️ Entry Tickets: Temples are mostly free; some cultural shows or museums may charge small fees. Boat rides can vary in price—negotiate or book in advance.
