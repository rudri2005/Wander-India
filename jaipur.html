Jaipur - The Pink City

The Pink City of India

Jaipur, the capital of Rajasthan and famously known as the “Pink City,” is a vibrant blend of royal heritage, architectural splendor, and cultural richness. Founded in 1727 by Maharaja Sawai Jai Singh II, the city was meticulously planned with the help of architect Vidyadhar Bhattacharya, following the principles of Vastu Shastra and Shilpa Shastra, making it one of India’s first planned cities. Designed to ease the growing population and water scarcity of the nearby Amber town, Jaipur features broad avenues, walled fortifications, and organized sectors. In 1876, the city was painted pink to welcome the Prince of Wales, a tradition that gave Jaipur its famous nickname. Today, Jaipur is a key part of India’s Golden Triangle tourist circuit and a UNESCO World Heritage Site, known for its majestic forts, palaces, colorful bazaars, and timeless charm that continues to attract visitors from around the world.As part of the Golden Triangle tourist circuit, Jaipur offers an unforgettable experience of India's regal past.
