Delhi - India's Capital City

Delhi: The Heart of India

Delhi, the capital of India, is a dynamic city where centuries of history meet modern life. Believed to be the site of the ancient city of Indraprastha from the Mahabharata, Delhi has been the seat of many powerful empires, including the Delhi Sultanate, the Mughals, and the British Raj. These rulers have left behind a rich architectural legacy seen in landmarks like the Red Fort, Jama Masjid, Qutub Minar, and India Gate. While Old Delhi preserves the charm of narrow lanes, bustling bazaars, and historic monuments, New Delhi showcases broad avenues, government buildings, and planned infrastructure. Today, Delhi is not only a political hub but also a cultural and economic powerhouse, reflecting the diverse spirit of India. Today, Delhi stands as a bustling metropolis known for its historical landmarks, diverse cuisine, and cultural vibrancy.

👜 Shopping: Explore local markets like Chandni Chowk, Sarojini Nagar, and Dilli Haat for clothes, spices, handicrafts, and souvenirs.
🚕 Transport: Use the Delhi Metro for fast travel. Auto-rickshaws and cabs are widely available; use apps like Ola or Uber for convenience.
⚠️ Safety: Stay alert in crowded areas, avoid isolated spots at night, and beware of pickpockets. Drink bottled water and check food hygiene.
📅 Best Time: October–March. Pleasant weather for sightseeing. Avoid May–June due to extreme heat.
🎟️ Entry Tickets: Major monuments like the Red Fort, Qutub Minar, and Humayun’s Tomb have online ticket options. Indian and foreigner prices may differ.
