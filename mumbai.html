Mumbai - City Of Dreams

The City of Dreams

Mumbai, the financial capital of India, is a fast-paced, vibrant metropolis located on the western coast along the Arabian Sea. Formerly known as Bombay, the city is a melting pot of cultures, dreams, and opportunities. It is home to Bollywood—the heart of India's film industry—as well as major corporate hubs, colonial-era architecture, bustling markets, and a rich street food culture. From the iconic Gateway of India and Marine Drive to the lively local trains and cosmopolitan lifestyle, Mumbai represents both tradition and modernity. Often called the "City That Never Sleeps," it draws people from across the country chasing careers, creativity, and a better life.

Fantastic Facts

Mumbai is home to India’s first and busiest railway station, Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus (CSMT), a UNESCO World Heritage Site known for its stunning Victorian Gothic architecture. It was completed in 1887 and serves over 3 million commuters daily, making it a vital transportation hub. The station's intricate design, combining elements of Indian and European architectural styles, makes it one of the most iconic landmarks in the city. It also holds historical significance, being a symbol of Mumbai's colonial past and its transition into a bustling metropolis.
