Udaipur - City Of Lakes

The City of Lakes and Royal Splendor

Udaipur, located in Rajasthan, is famously known as the “City of Lakes” and is one of India’s most romantic and picturesque destinations. Founded in 1559 by Maharana Udai Singh II, the city is surrounded by the Aravalli Hills and dotted with beautiful lakes, palaces, and temples. The stunning City Palace, sitting on the banks of Lake Pichola, showcases the rich heritage of the Mewar dynasty with its intricate architecture and royal artifacts. Udaipur’s charm lies in its serene boat rides, lively bazaars, traditional Rajasthani culture, and luxurious heritage hotels that make it a popular choice for travelers and destination weddings alike. The City Palace—a massive complex of courtyards, balconies, and towers—overlooks Lake Pichola and is a testament to the grandeur of Rajput architecture. Nearby, the Lake Palace, now a luxury hotel, floats like a dream in the middle of the lake. Udaipur is also home to cultural gems like the Jagdish Temple, Bagore Ki Haveli, and the scenic Sajjangarh Monsoon Palace.

Fantastic Facts

Udaipur is home to the stunning Lake Palace, one of the most iconic and luxurious heritage hotels in the world. Originally built in 1746 by Maharana Jagat Singh II, this white marble palace was once a royal summer retreat for the Mewar dynasty. It is situated on Jag Niwas Island in the middle of Lake Pichola and appears to float magically on water.What makes the Lake Palace even more special is its unique blend of Mughal and Rajput architecture, featuring intricate carvings, courtyards, fountains, and open terraces with panoramic views. Today, it operates as a five-star hotel under the Taj Group, offering guests a royal experience with boat rides, candle-lit dinners by the lake, and views of the majestic City Palace and Aravalli hills.The palace gained international fame when it was featured in the James Bond film “Octopussy” (1983) and continues to be a symbol of romance, luxury, and Rajasthan's royal heritage.

👜 Shopping: Shop for Banarasi silk sarees, wooden toys, perfumes (ittar), and brassware. Best places: Vishwanath Gali, Thatheri Bazaar.
🚕 Transport: Use auto-rickshaws or walk in the narrow lanes. Cycle rickshaws and boats are popular near the ghats. Avoid driving yourself.
⚠️ Safety: Keep belongings secure, beware of pickpockets in crowded areas, and politely decline persistent touts. Drink bottled water only.
📅 Best Time: November–February. Pleasant weather and major festivals like Dev Deepawali make it ideal for visits.
🎟️ Entry Tickets: Temples are mostly free; some cultural shows or museums may charge small fees. Boat rides can vary in price—negotiate or book in advance.
